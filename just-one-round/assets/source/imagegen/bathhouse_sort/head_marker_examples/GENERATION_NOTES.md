## Shared prompt contract

- Preserve the orange tabby identity, face, sleepy-silly expression, proportions, front-facing pose, sandals, stepped dark-brown outline, lighting, and polished high-resolution pixel-inspired rendering.
- Show exactly two equal-scale full-body copies on the same baseline: blue on the left and red on the right.
- Preserve the thin cotton-linen ryokan robe, long lapels, broad short sleeves, thin off-center side ties, and checked lining.
- Change only robe color and the specified head marker.
- Use a perfectly flat `#FF00FF` background with no floor, shadow, UI, divider, labels, scenery, or extra characters.
