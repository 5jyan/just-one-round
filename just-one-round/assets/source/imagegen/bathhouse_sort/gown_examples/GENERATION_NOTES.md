# Bathhouse gown examples

Reference: `../animal_guests/raw-master.png`

Shared constraints for every image:

- Preserve the orange tabby character, expression, proportions, pose, sandals, framing, outlines, lighting, and polished high-resolution pixel-inspired rendering.
- Change clothing only; keep both ears visible and add no headwear.
- Use a blue western bathrobe with blue collar, cuffs, and self-fabric belt.
- Exclude gi, judo/karate uniform, yukata, kimono, white trim, white X-shaped collar, and martial-arts belt.
- Keep one centered full-body character on a uniform `#FF00FF` background with no scenery, text, UI, or shadows.

## Variants

1. `01-smooth-cotton-hotel-robe.png`: soft matte woven cotton, rounded shawl collar, broad folds, patch pockets, relaxed hotel-bathrobe silhouette.
2. `02-waffle-spa-robe.png`: lightweight cotton with restrained large-scale square waffle weave and a narrower spa-robe collar.
3. `03-microfleece-robe.png`: brushed microfiber fleece with broad plush shading and smooth low-frequency nap.
4. `04-hooded-bathrobe.png`: smooth matte cotton, empty hood behind the shoulders, minimal front overlap, no zipper or buttons.
5. `05-ryokan-yukata-loungewear.png`: thin indigo cotton-linen, long lapels, broad short sleeves, off-center side ties, and subtle checked lining based on the supplied Japanese loungewear reference.

These are comparison sources only and are not connected to the runtime atlas.
