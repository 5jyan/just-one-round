# Sausage Dachshund master module contract

- Raw grid: 2 rows × 3 columns
- Runtime cell: 32 × 32 px
- Display scale: 1× logical cell, nearest-neighbor
- Art density: effective 2 × 2 logical pixel clusters
- Body width: approximately 18–20 px
- Dog anatomy: two front legs in the head module, two rear legs in the tail module
- Body geometry: straight horizontal/vertical modules and compact one-cell elbows
- Connectors: centered on cell edges, identical width, continuous fill and outline
- Sausage: unwrapped grilled meat with blunt ends and dark grill marks
- Transparency: chroma-keyed from flat `#FF00FF`

The master sheet is an identity and assembly test. Animation and directional sheets
must preserve its palette, outline weight, body width, camera, and anatomy.
