## Pass

- The long muzzle, low-set floppy ears, black nose, short legs, and tapering tail read as a dachshund.
- The body is one organic elongated torso rather than repeated sausage or centipede segments.
- Exactly four legs are shown.
- No clothing or garment-like stripes remain on the dog.
- The sausage pickup uses blunt meat ends, grill marks, and steam instead of candy-wrapper twists.
- Straight and corner body sections follow individual grid cells without obvious visual overhang into adjacent cells.
- The neck joins the shoulder without a visible gap or collar-like cut line.
- The long torso has no internal tile seams or color breaks.
- Broad S-curves and intestine-like coils are absent; the visible run is predominantly straight with a compact elbow.
- Exactly two front legs remain at the shoulder and two rear legs remain at the hips, with no middle-body paws.
- The sausage pickup remains brighter and visually separate from the brown fur.
- The dog, pickup, grid, environment, and HUD follow a clear contrast hierarchy.
- The midnight kitchen palette and chunky pixel treatment are coherent with the inspected project references.
- The board remains mostly clear and the Snake-like path is immediately understandable.
